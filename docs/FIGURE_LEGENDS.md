# Figure legends

**Figure 1 | Multiome comparison and TSS-centred chromatin context.** (A) Four same-nucleus RNA/ATAC libraries from two human muscle source lines contribute 958 depth-matched COQ8A-high (≥2 RNA UMI) versus low (1 UMI) nucleus pairs under the primary ATAC TSS-enrichment ≥3 gate. (B) Mean strand-oriented ATAC Tn5 insertion profile within ±5 kb of one GENCODE v48 TSS for each of the 221 measured Hallmark/Reactome myogenesis genes. (C) Corresponding 221-row TSS heatmaps, with one shared absolute colour scale and gene rows sorted by pooled high+low TSS signal. Both groups have 958 nuclei. Fragment cut sites receive the +4/−5 bp Tn5 shift, are binned every 100 bp, and undergo Gaussian smoothing for display. The profile is descriptive and separate from the ±100-kb candidate-peak screen. The all-window high/low cut ratio is 1.0066.

**Figure 2 | Complete myogenesis-space screen and target nomination.** (A) All 6,871 evaluable peaks among 7,132 candidates near the fixed 221 genes; x-axis is the COQ8A-high minus low open-nucleus percentage-point difference and y-axis the nominal paired peak-test −log10 p. (B) All 221 gene-region ATAC summaries on analogous axes; MYOD1 is highlighted for orientation. Annotation in A and B states the minimum Benjamini–Hochberg q over the corresponding complete test family; neither family has q<0.05. (C) Paired normalized-RNA gene effect versus nearby ATAC gene-region effect for all 221 genes, with MYOD1 highlighted. (D) The ten highest of all 221 genes under an **exploratory** same-data ranking: the number of positive peak–RNA links whose ATAC effect is positive in at least three of four libraries. MYOD1 ranks first, with five such links. The ranking and highlight use overlapping nuclei with A–C; they nominate a locus and are not an independent confirmation. The biological source-line count is two, and nucleus-pair p values do not represent donor-level replication.

**Supplementary Figure QC | Joint ATAC quality and paired-depth balance.** (A) TSS enrichment distributions for the four libraries after initial depth filters; the dashed line is the primary TSS≥3 threshold. (B,C) Total RNA UMI and open ATAC peak counts, respectively, in each matched high/low pair after log1p transform. The dotted diagonal is equality. This verifies matching on two measured depths; it does not itself remove all biological or technical confounding.

**Figure 3 | MYOD1 locus and exploratory sensitivity.** (A) All 19 four-library-common peaks near MYOD1 along hg38 chr11. Bar height is the primary COQ8A≥2 minus 1 UMI open-fraction difference; red denotes the six positive MYOD1 peak–RNA links passing the exploratory MRF PWM-score <0.95 rule when links are learned at TSS≥2. (B) Open-fraction high/low ratios for five region sets under both ≥2 and ≥3 UMI contrasts, with effect testing at TSS≥3. (C) Four library effects for the six-region, ≥3 versus 1 UMI contrast. (D) Pair exclusion by the upper tail of RNA Scrublet score. The six-region effect is 13.27% versus 10.36% open, ratio 1.280, in 201 matched pairs (nominal paired p=0.0256); the ratio falls to 1.192 after top-2.5% RNA doublet-score exclusion. The 20 region/QC settings are sensitivity checks, not a family of independent discoveries. The six peaks were selected from overlapping nuclei and are a hypothesis for follow-up.
