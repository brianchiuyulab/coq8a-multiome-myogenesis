# Figure legends

**Figure 1 | Knowledge-based myogenesis search in the same-nucleus multiome.** (A) Study design and the defined biological search space. RNA and ATAC measurements came from each nucleus in four 10x Multiome libraries from two source lines at stem and differentiated states. Joint QC and within-library depth matching yield 958 primary pairs. The 221 measured Hallmark∪Reactome Myogenesis genes define candidate regions ±100 kb from transcript TSS, with no motif filter. (B) Paired RNA programme differences for COQ8A≥2 versus 1 UMI with 95% nucleus-pair intervals. (C) Paired mean differences in nearby common-peak accessibility, expressed in percentage points. Labels report BH q over the four programme summaries and count of positive libraries. (D) All 221 gene-level normalized RNA effects versus all-four-common-nearby-peak ATAC effects. Selected named genes are labels, not the search boundary. Nucleus-level inference is descriptive because there are only two independent source lines.

**Figure 2 | Complete primary ATAC search space.** (A) Every evaluable candidate peak in the fixed 221-gene myogenesis search space, with MYOD1-nearby peaks highlighted only for orientation. The plot does not filter peaks on MRF motif or COQ8A effect. (B) Mean nearby-common-peak ATAC effect for all 221 genes. Axes show unadjusted paired nucleus-level p values to display the distribution; annotation gives the minimum BH-adjusted q over each whole family. Neither family has q<0.05.

**Figure 3 | Same-nucleus display of the primary matched comparison.** (A) Joint RNA/ATAC UMAP of 1,916 nuclei from 958 matched pairs, coloured by the two source lines and stem/differentiated libraries. The embedding combines 2,000 label-blind variable RNA genes with 5,097 all-four-common peaks in the fixed myogenesis search space. (B) The same coordinates coloured by COQ8A UMI-defined group; no cell-type or trajectory labels are inferred. (C,D) Per-nucleus Hallmark Myogenesis RNA score and mean gene-level nearby ATAC open fraction, respectively, by library and COQ8A group. Black points mark medians. The displayed programme values reproduce the primary paired differences; violin shapes are descriptive. (E) Regulator RNA dot plot: dot area denotes detection frequency and colour denotes the within-gene standardized mean log-normalized expression. (F) Fraction of high/low nuclei open at each of 19 four-library-common peaks near MYOD1. These are peak-level fractions from the filtered matrices, not continuous fragment coverage. This visual organization was informed by the multiome figure in [Martini et al. (Nature, 2026)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10791-2); the present comparison is observational and no histone-modification data are plotted.

**Supplementary Figure QC | Joint ATAC quality and paired-depth balance.** (A) TSS enrichment distributions for the four libraries after initial depth filters; the dashed line is the primary TSS≥3 threshold. (B,C) Total RNA UMI and open ATAC peak counts, respectively, in each matched high/low pair after log1p transform. The dotted diagonal is equality. This verifies matching on two measured depths; it does not itself remove all biological or technical confounding.

**Supplementary Figure MYOD1 Exploratory | Locus discovery and threshold sensitivity.** (A) Every one of 19 MYOD1 nearby peaks shared across four libraries along hg38 chr11, positioned by peak midpoint. Stem height is the COQ8A≥2 minus 1 UMI difference in open-nucleus percentage points. Outline denotes a positive same-data peak–RNA MYOD1 link discovered at TSS≥2, and a star marks a linked peak with maximum MRF PWM score <0.95. These are computational associations, not experimentally assigned enhancers. (B) High/low open-fraction ratios for five MYOD1 region sets with COQ8A≥2 or ≥3 versus 1 UMI at TSS≥3: 32 candidates, 19 common, ten TSS≥3 links, five MRF-score<0.95 TSS≥3 links, and six MRF-score<0.95 TSS≥2 links. Pair-level p values are shown beside points. The complete 20 combinations, including TSS≥2 for the effect test, are supplied in the sensitivity table. (C) Four separate library effects for the six TSS≥2-linked MRF-score<0.95 peaks under the ≥3 extreme contrast, with 95% nucleus-pair intervals and matched-pair counts. Linked regions were selected in overlapping nuclei; these p values are exploratory.
