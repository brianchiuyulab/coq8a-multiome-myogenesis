# Figure legends

**Figure 1 | From the fixed myogenesis programme to an exploratory MYOD1 locus.** (A) The 221 measured Hallmark/Reactome genes define 7,132 unique candidate peaks within ±100 kb of any transcript TSS; 6,871 satisfy the pooled prevalence test floor. Neither the peak nor 221 gene-region family has a BH q<0.05 discovery. (B) Mean ATAC Tn5 cut profile and two 221-row heatmaps at ±5 kb from the GENCODE v48 gene-feature TSS. The low and high groups each contain one nucleus from each of the 958 primary within-library depth-matched pairs (COQ8A≥2 versus 1 UMI; TSS enrichment≥3). Indexed deduplicated fragments were counted as two cut sites per fragment with +4/−5 bp shifts, oriented to gene strand, binned every 100 bp and smoothed across adjacent bins with a 100-bp Gaussian kernel for display. Both heatmaps have one shared absolute colour scale; rows are ordered by pooled high+low TSS signal, independently of the high–low difference. The red line marks MYOD1, not a threshold. This TSS display is descriptive and is **not** the ±100-kb peak-based ranking or six-peak test. (C) The top ten of all 221 genes ranked by the number of same-data positive RNA links whose peaks have higher accessibility in ≥3/4 libraries. MYOD1 ranks first; this ranking is exploratory. (D) MYOD1 has 32 nearby candidates, 19 all-four-library common peaks and 11 positive links when the TSS≥2 link-discovery gate is used; six of those links meet the exploratory MRF-score<0.95 rule. Those six peaks give 13.27% versus 10.36% open nuclei (ratio 1.280) among 201 pairs under the stronger COQ8A≥3 versus 1 contrast at TSS≥3. This is a same-data, threshold-sensitive locus hypothesis, not an FDR-positive primary discovery. The biological source-line count is two.

**Figure 2 | Complete primary ATAC search space.** (A) Every evaluable candidate peak in the fixed 221-gene myogenesis search space. The plot does not filter or highlight peaks on motif, gene name or COQ8A effect. (B) Mean nearby-common-peak ATAC effect for all 221 genes. Axes show unadjusted paired nucleus-level p values to display the distribution; annotation gives the minimum BH-adjusted q over each whole family. Neither family has q<0.05. The subsequent MYOD1 view is separately labelled exploratory in the supplement.

**Supplementary Figure QC | Joint ATAC quality and paired-depth balance.** (A) TSS enrichment distributions for the four libraries after initial depth filters; the dashed line is the primary TSS≥3 threshold. (B,C) Total RNA UMI and open ATAC peak counts, respectively, in each matched high/low pair after log1p transform. The dotted diagonal is equality. This verifies matching on two measured depths; it does not itself remove all biological or technical confounding.

**Supplementary Figure MYOD1 Exploratory | Locus discovery and threshold sensitivity.** (A) Every one of 19 MYOD1 nearby peaks shared across four libraries along hg38 chr11, positioned by peak midpoint. Stem height is the COQ8A≥2 minus 1 UMI difference in open-nucleus percentage points. Outline denotes a positive same-data peak–RNA MYOD1 link discovered at TSS≥2, and a star marks a linked peak with maximum MRF PWM score <0.95. These are computational associations, not experimentally assigned enhancers. (B) High/low open-fraction ratios for five MYOD1 region sets with COQ8A≥2 or ≥3 versus 1 UMI at TSS≥3: 32 candidates, 19 common, ten TSS≥3 links, five MRF-score<0.95 TSS≥3 links, and six MRF-score<0.95 TSS≥2 links. Pair-level p values are shown beside points. The complete 20 combinations, including TSS≥2 for the effect test, are supplied in the sensitivity table. (C) Four separate library effects for the six TSS≥2-linked MRF-score<0.95 peaks under the ≥3 extreme contrast, with 95% nucleus-pair intervals and matched-pair counts. Linked regions were selected in overlapping nuclei; these p values are exploratory.
