# Figure legends

**Figure 1 | Main-comparison design and TSS-centred ATAC context.** (A) Four same-nucleus RNA/ATAC libraries from two human muscle source lines provide 201 within-library depth-matched COQ8A ≥3 versus 1 RNA UMI nucleus pairs (46/74/58/23) at ATAC TSS enrichment ≥3. (B) Mean strand-oriented Tn5 insertion profile within ±5 kb of one GENCODE v48 TSS for each of the 221 measured Hallmark/Reactome myogenesis genes. (C) Corresponding 221-row heatmaps, ordered by pooled high+low TSS signal and shown on one shared absolute colour scale. Fragment cut sites receive +4/−5 bp shifts, are binned every 100 bp and smoothed for display. The total high/low TSS-cut ratio is 1.0162. This profile is descriptive and separate from the ±100-kb candidate-peak screen.

**Figure 2 | Parallel complete myogenesis-space screens at both COQ8A count thresholds.** (A) All 6,871 prevalence-eligible peaks in the ≥2-versus-1 contrast (958 matched pairs). (B) All 3,731 prevalence-eligible peaks in the ≥3-versus-1 contrast (201 pairs). Both come from 7,132 candidate peaks around the same fixed 221 genes. Axes show high-minus-low opening in percentage points and nominal paired-test −log10 p; annotations give minimum whole-family BH q. (C) ATAC effects for **all 221 gene regions** under both contrasts, with MYOD1 and CKB highlighted; the minimum 221-gene BH q is 0.302 at ≥2 and 0.145 at ≥3, with none <0.05. MYOD1's 19-common-peak ratios are 1.0688 and 1.0934, respectively. (D) Exploratory same-data ranking under each contrast: number of positive peak–RNA links whose ATAC effect rises in at least three of four libraries. Links were learned in ≥2-versus-1 nuclei at TSS≥3; ATAC direction is recalculated for each displayed contrast. MYOD1 ranks first at ≥2 (five links); CKB ranks first and MYOD1 second at ≥3 (four and three links). The biological source-line count is two. The ≥3 local six-peak comparison in Figure 3 follows a displayed complete ≥3 screen, not an unlabelled switch from A.

**Figure 3 | MYOD1 locus and exploratory sensitivity.** (A) All 19 four-library-common MYOD1-nearby peaks along hg38 chr11; bar height is the **≥3-versus-1** high-minus-low opening difference. Red denotes six positive MYOD1 peak–RNA links passing an exploratory MRF PWM-score <0.95 rule when links are learned at TSS≥2. (B) High/low ratios for five region sets under the ≥3 main and ≥2 sensitivity contrasts, with effect testing at TSS≥3. (C) Four library effects for the six-region ≥3-versus-1 contrast. (D) Pair exclusion by upper RNA Scrublet-score tail. The six-region result is 13.267% versus 10.365% open (ratio 1.280; 201 pairs; nominal paired p=0.0256), falling to 1.192 after top-2.5% RNA doublet-score exclusion. The region sets were learned in overlapping nuclei, so the p value is descriptive; the 20 settings examine stability and are not a separate BH test family.

**Supplementary Figure QC | Joint ATAC quality and matched-depth balance.** (A) TSS-enrichment distributions for the four libraries after depth filtering; the dashed line marks TSS≥3. (B,C) Matched high/low total RNA UMI and open ATAC peak counts after log1p transform in the same **201 main-contrast pairs**. The dotted diagonal is equality.
