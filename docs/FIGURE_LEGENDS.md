# Figure legends

**Figure 1 | Knowledge-based myogenesis search in the same-nucleus multiome.** (A) Study and outcome-independent search space. RNA and ATAC measurements came from each nucleus in four 10x Multiome libraries from two source lines at stem and differentiated states. Joint QC and within-library depth matching yield 958 primary pairs. The 221 measured Hallmark∪Reactome Myogenesis genes define candidate regions ±100 kb from transcript TSS, with no motif filter. (B) Paired RNA programme differences for COQ8A≥2 versus 1 UMI with 95% nucleus-pair intervals. (C) Paired mean differences in nearby common-peak accessibility, expressed in percentage points. Labels report BH q over the four programme summaries and count of positive libraries. (D) All 221 gene-level normalized RNA effects versus all-four-common-nearby-peak ATAC effects. Selected named genes are labels, not the search boundary. Nucleus-level inference is descriptive because there are only two independent source lines.

**Figure 2 | MYOD1 locus discovery and threshold sensitivity.** (A) Every one of 19 MYOD1 nearby peaks shared across four libraries along hg38 chr11, positioned by peak midpoint. Stem height is the COQ8A≥2 minus 1 UMI difference in open-nucleus percentage points. Outline denotes a positive same-data peak–RNA MYOD1 link discovered at TSS≥2, and a star marks a linked peak without a strong MRF motif score. These are computational associations, not experimentally assigned enhancers. (B) High/low open-fraction ratios for five MYOD1 region sets with COQ8A≥2 or ≥3 versus 1 UMI at TSS≥3: 32 candidates, 19 common, ten TSS≥3 links, five weak-MRF TSS≥3 links, and six weak-MRF TSS≥2 links. Pair-level p values are shown beside points. The complete 20 combinations, including TSS≥2 for the effect test, are supplied in the sensitivity table. (C) Four separate library effects for the six TSS≥2-linked weak-MRF peaks under the ≥3 extreme contrast, with 95% nucleus-pair intervals and matched-pair counts. Linked regions were selected in overlapping nuclei; these p values are exploratory.

**Supplementary Figure QC | Joint ATAC quality and paired-depth balance.** (A) TSS enrichment distributions for the four libraries after initial depth filters; the dashed line is the primary TSS≥3 threshold. (B,C) Total RNA UMI and open ATAC peak counts, respectively, in each matched high/low pair after log1p transform. The dotted diagonal is equality. This verifies matching on two measured depths; it does not itself remove all biological or technical confounding.

**Supplementary Figure Global ATAC Scan | The complete primary test families.** (A) Every evaluable ATAC candidate peak in the fixed 221-gene myogenesis search space, with MYOD1-nearby peaks highlighted only for orientation. The plot does not filter peaks on MRF motif or COQ8A effect. (B) Mean nearby-common-peak ATAC effect for all 221 genes. Axes show unadjusted paired nucleus-level p values to display distribution; annotation gives the minimum BH-adjusted q over each whole family. Neither family has q<0.05.
